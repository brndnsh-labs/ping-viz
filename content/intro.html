What happens when you type ping google.com and press Enter?

It's an old interview question and a good one, because there's no bottom to it. You can answer in a sentence or talk for an hour, and either way you'll leave something out.

This is the long answer, drawn. One ping, from the key switch closing to the reply reaching your eyes, with a clock in the corner showing how much real time has passed. Then the same question for a prompt to Claude.

Scroll to press Enter. Scrolling is time here: some chapters cover twenty microseconds, others ten minutes.
