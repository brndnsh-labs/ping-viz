What happens when you type ping google.com and press Enter?

ping is a small program that sends a message to another computer and times how long the reply takes to come back. Asking what happens when you run it is a variation on an old interview question, which usually asks about typing google.com into a browser. The ping version strips it down to one message and its reply, simple enough to follow the whole way. There's still no bottom to it. You can answer in a sentence or talk for an hour, and either way you'll leave something out.

This is the long answer, drawn. One ping, from the key switch closing to the reply reaching your eyes, with a clock in the corner showing how much real time has passed. Then the same question for a prompt to Claude.

Scroll to press Enter. Scrolling is time here: some chapters cover twenty microseconds (millionths of a second), others ten minutes.
